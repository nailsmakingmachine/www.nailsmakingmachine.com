Home / Nail Making Machine

High-Performance Automated Nail Manufacturing Systems

Nails Making Machine engineers heavy-duty automatic wire nail making machines capable of manufacturing 0.5-inch to 6-inch steel nails at speeds up to 700 pieces per minute. Built in Rajkot, Gujarat, our machinery incorporates robust cast iron bodies, precision-ground cams, and automated oil lubrication matrices to guarantee continuous round-the-clock factory output.

Whether setting up a new nail manufacturing unit or upgrading existing production lines, our turnkey solutions deliver lower power consumption, minimal wire wastage, and consistent nail dimensional accuracy.
